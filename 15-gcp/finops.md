# GCP FinOps

FinOps combines cloud engineering, finance, and operational practices to improve cloud cost visibility and efficiency.

## Areas to Review

- Billing reports
- Budgets
- Cost allocation
- Labels and tags
- Rightsizing
- Idle resources
- Committed use discounts
- Storage lifecycle
- Resource utilization
- Cost anomalies

## Cost Optimization Flow

Cloud Spend
   |
Cost Visibility
   |
Allocation
   |
Utilization
   |
Optimization Opportunity
   |
Engineering Review
   |
Implementation
   |
Savings Validation

## Common Opportunities

- Oversized compute
- Idle VMs
- Unused disks
- Old snapshots
- Unused IP addresses
- Inefficient storage classes
- Missing labels or tags
- Stable workloads suitable for commitments

## Important Principle

Do not optimize solely based on cost.

Consider:

- Reliability
- Performance
- Capacity
- Business requirements
- Growth
- Disaster recovery
